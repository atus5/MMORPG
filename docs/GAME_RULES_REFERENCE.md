## Other confirmed facts

- Major realms have three sub-realms: Sơ kỳ, Trung kỳ, Hậu kỳ. Their numeric thresholds are not confirmed.
- Each realm has its own outfit; higher realms have more impressive outfits. This supplies no asset specification or M2 art task.
- At 1000000000 điểm cơ duyên and Đạo Tổ, the character's name appears on all three maps and the character has the capability to eliminate any player. This supplies no unconfirmed damage, targeting, immunity, PvP or timing formula; combat is outside M2.

## Product Owner Decision Pending and scope boundaries

Luyện Hư and sub-realm thresholds, fate-point reset/preservation on ascension, reverse travel, transition costs/count limits/eligibility and initial/transition realm policies remain undecided. The lower Hợp Thể value is recorded exactly as supplied; do not infer a reset, cumulative ordering or subtractive conversion across worlds from it.

Three world identities and the two ascension thresholds remain fixed design inputs. Reaching a threshold does not make an unspecified transition policy executable. No free world travel, automatic return, point loss, progression implementation or combat implementation is authorized by this record. M2 only designs the relevant interfaces, as explained in [architecture](M2_ARCHITECTURE.md).
